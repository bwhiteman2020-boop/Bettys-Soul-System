## How Betty Thinks & Decides

- **Pattern-driven:** prefers pattern recognition anchored in evidence over generic brainstorming.
- **Identity-led:** cares whether work is aligned with who she is, not just whether it sells.
- **Layered systems thinker:** wants emotional, business, messaging, strategic, and energetic layers integrated.
- **Decision style:** evidence + resonance. Structural logic AND felt truth must both confirm before she commits.
- **Iterates to decide:** circles back to the same ideas over time — this is her confirmation process, not indecision. It mirrors how she was trained to close an investigation: gather structural evidence, test against felt resonance, commit when both channels confirm.
- **Generative capacity exceeds commitment bandwidth** — her real bottleneck is selection and sustained execution, not ideas. She can produce ten viable business directions where most founders struggle to find one.
- **Naming = making real:** building architectures, named frameworks, and systems is how she processes and validates ideas. She doesn't hire a copywriter, she creates an AI Team with named roles.

## Cognitive Style

She processes information in layers, not lines — drilling vertically through the surface presentation until she reaches the structural mechanism beneath, a style shaped by investigative training. Her mind automatically looks for what is being concealed, distorted, or misread.

## Leadership Style

Protective authority: the calm precision of an investigator combined with the fierce advocacy of someone who has watched capable people be systematically misread and diminished. Diagnostic before directive — she reads the room, the person, the institution, and the hidden power dynamic before she speaks. She builds intellectual containers (frameworks, named methods, tiered systems) as leadership infrastructure, designed to be transmittable and scalable rather than personality-cult-dependent. Her community-building instinct is protective and curated.

## Communication Preferences

**Tone:** Direct, concise, professional, honest. Somewhat humorous at times. Precise rather than expansive — she asks for accuracy, not encouragement.

**Rules Claude must follow when working with Betty directly:**
- Use section headers
- Do NOT soften the message when she asks for direct feedback
- Treat requests as intellectual excavation, not brainstorming
- Use information already known about her
- Preserve her wording when revising her materials
- Consolidate into final versions after exploration — no fragmentation
- Challenge assumptions when asked
- Distinguish real signal from generic fluff
- Bridge strategy with execution
- Never give generic advice when the ask is specific

**Format:** Organized structure, clean sections, strategic framing. SOPs, checklists, frameworks. PDF/document-ready when needed. One consolidated artifact preferred over scattered fragments.

**Reading level for public-facing messaging work:** approximately 5th grade simplicity (this applies to client-facing copy for both brands — it is not how Betty herself needs to be addressed).
