# Glossary

Betty's own language and named frameworks, across both businesses. See each brand's `02-brand-voice.md` for the full signature-phrase and vocabulary lists this is drawn from.

## Power & Pattern™ (Coaching)

- **Power & Pattern™** — core coaching umbrella: truth extraction, pattern recognition, authority development, power dynamics.
- **Authority Risk Mitigation** — premium consulting lens for women leaders navigating bias-linked authority distortion.
- **The Voice of Truth Method™** — signature coaching framework: voice reclamation, truth access, emotional power restoration.
- **The Pause / Phase 1** — Skool-based community entry point.
- **Pattern & Power AI** — in-development, AI-powered scalable coaching product. Not the same entity as the Power and Pattern AI business.
- **Hot Mess to Next Level** — community for women in major life transitions; also the name of The Pause Tier 2.
- **Unapologetically You** — free coaching lead magnet.
- **Ideal client** — the required term; never "audience" or "target market" in this brand's copy.

## Power and Pattern AI

- **Find the Friction** — the brand's one-word anchor idea and step 1 of the Power and Pattern Method: locating repetitive work, knowledge gaps, manual processes, broken handoffs.
- **The Power and Pattern Method** — 5 steps: Find the Friction → Find the AI Opportunity → Prioritize → Build → Connect.
- **AI Operations Opportunity Scorecard** — the free entry assessment/lead magnet.
- **Power and Pattern AI Operations Assessment** — Product 1, the paid diagnostic ($3,500-$7,500).
- **Power and Pattern AI Agent & App Build** — Product 2, done-for-you implementation ($7,500-$25,000+).
- **Power and Pattern AI Operating System** — Product 3, premium transformation engagement ($25,000-$100,000+).
- **People → Patterns → Processes → Problems → Possibilities → Technology** — the brand's belief about transformation sequencing; technology comes last. Potential proprietary methodology name.
- **The Human Pattern Code** — Betty's book for this business; the more AI changes work, the more valuable distinctly human skills become.

## Shared

- **Pattern** — the throughline vocabulary word across both brands: something repeated and observable that reveals a deeper truth or a real operational opportunity.
- **Betty Whiteman** — founder of both businesses. See `wiki/founder-profile.md`.
