# Power & Pattern™ (Coaching) — Market & Competitors

Source: `pattern-and-power-starter-kit` skill, `references/competitive-landscape.md`.

## Market Position Profile

- **Industry / Niche:** Strategic advisory and coaching at the intersection of investigative behavioral intelligence, institutional power dynamics, and authority development for Black women leaders 40+.
- **Sub-niche:** Authority Risk Mitigation — protecting and restoring authority for high-performing women facing bias-linked authority distortion inside institutions and corporate environments. Betty is the only advisor in this space with a background as a police detective, behavioral analyst, and HR Director inside higher education.
- **Market Position:** Premium boutique authority in a space underserved by serious, diagnostics-based advisory. Most competitors offer coaching, community, or motivation. Betty offers a framework, a diagnosis, and a protection strategy — a strategic diagnostician, not a peer support provider.
- **Geographic Focus:** Global English-speaking; US-primary. Fully remote/virtual delivery.
- **Platform Presence:** LinkedIn (primary thought leadership), Substack (long-form authority content), Skool (community), Instagram (brand awareness), podcasts as a guest (distribution).
