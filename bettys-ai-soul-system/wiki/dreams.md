A million-dollar business within roughly one year.

## Most Likely Long-Term Form

A thought leader with a named methodology, a premium advisory practice, an AI-powered product that extends her reach, and a body of intellectual property that functions independently of her time — across both the Power & Pattern™ coaching umbrella and Power and Pattern AI.

## The Single Variable

Not a lack of ideas, expertise, or vision. The pace of the whole trajectory is determined by one thing: commitment to a visible flagship, followed by sustained public execution. The expertise is fully formed. What is being built now is the platform that makes it undeniable.

## Motivational Drivers Behind the Dream

1. **Legacy through codification** — a body of work that outlasts any single client engagement.
2. **Vindication through visibility** — making the value she carries undeniable.
3. **Protective impact** — serving the specific people who have been systematically underserved: Black women 40+ (coaching), and operations leaders drowning in manual work and AI confusion (AI consulting).
