## Content Pillars

1. **Find the Friction** — recognizing inefficient operations (work that shouldn't be manual, knowledge bottleneck signs, hidden costs, "the Sarah knows everything problem")
2. **AI Use Cases** — practical examples (knowledge assistants, policy bots, SOP assistants, reporting agents, intake apps, employee support agents, workflow automations)
3. **AI Strategy** — smarter AI decisions (what to automate first, why software isn't a strategy, prioritization, build vs. buy, when NOT to use AI, why the first project should be small)
4. **Operational Transformation** — connecting AI to outcomes (reducing admin burden, increasing capacity, employee experience, preserving institutional knowledge, consistency, scaling without headcount)
5. **AI Reality Checks** — challenging common beliefs (AI won't fix a broken process, you don't need 50 AI tools, ChatGPT isn't a strategy, automation isn't always AI, AI doesn't have to replace employees, the best AI solution may be an internal tool customers never see)
