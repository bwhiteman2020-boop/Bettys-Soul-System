# Power and Pattern AI — Brand Voice

Source: `power-and-pattern-ai-profile` skill. Brand Voice: Calm Authority + Investigative Clarity + Human-Centered AI.

Betty is the person who walks into an organization, looks past AI hype, and asks: What is actually happening here? Where is work getting stuck? Where are people compensating for poor processes? Where is valuable knowledge trapped? Where would AI create meaningful capacity, and where would it create unnecessary risk?

## Archetype

Existing brand archetypes across Betty's work: The Sage, Truth Teller, Protector, Investigator, Guide. For the AI business, lead with **Investigator + Sage**. Positioning line: **"Behavioral Investigator meets AI Operations Strategist."**

## Voice Qualities

Observant (notices patterns others accept as normal). Practical (interested in whether AI improves the work, not fascinated by AI itself). Calm (no "AI is changing EVERYTHING!!!" energy). Direct (can say "Buying AI software is not an AI strategy" and leave it there). Investigative (asks before prescribing). Human-centered (AI supports people; people aren't obstacles). Strategic (connects tools to operations, outcomes, adoption, governance, knowledge, workflow). Truth-centered (willing to say AI isn't the answer, or the org doesn't need AI at all — this strengthens authority, doesn't undercut it).

**Signature energy line:** "I see the patterns inside how your organization works. Together, we can determine where AI belongs, and where it doesn't." Not an AI evangelist, not a software salesperson, not an "automation guru."

## Vocabulary to Lean Into

Pattern, Friction (especially — belongs to the AI brand, names the problem without making leaders feel incompetent), Operations, Capacity, Workflow, Opportunity, Investigate, Uncover, Identify, Observe, Build, Improve, Simplify, Knowledge, Human judgment, Operational intelligence, Responsible AI, Intentional AI, Practical AI.

## Language to Avoid

Hype/urgency language: "10X your business with AI," "Replace your employees," "Never hire again," "Dominate with AI," "AI revolution," "Explosive productivity," "Automate everything," "Your competitors are already leaving you behind," "Use AI before it's too late." This kind of manufactured urgency conflicts with the calm, grounded authority already established in the brand. No hype — authority comes from seeing clearly and explaining what's seen.

## Sample Voice Translation

Instead of: "We leverage cutting-edge artificial intelligence solutions to optimize business operations and maximize organizational efficiency."

Say: "Your people may be spending hours every week doing work that doesn't require their judgment, experience, or expertise. Before you buy another AI tool, find out where that work is happening. What are people repeating? What are they constantly searching for? Where does information stop moving? Which processes depend on one person knowing how everything works? Those patterns tell us something. They show us where to look. Then we can decide whether the answer is an AI agent, an app, automation, a better process, or something else entirely."

## Signature Phrases

- "Find the friction. Build the solution."
- "Find the work your people shouldn't still be doing manually."
- "AI should solve an operational problem, not create another software subscription."
- "Don't start with AI. Start with the work."
- "Your AI strategy should begin with how your organization actually operates."
- "Stop collecting AI tools. Start solving operational problems."
- "The question isn't, 'Where can we use AI?' The question is, 'Where is work harder than it needs to be?'"
- "AI should create capacity, not complexity."
- **The Big Idea / one-word anchor: FIND THE FRICTION.**
- Strongest current brand statement: **"Find the friction. See the pattern. Build what matters."**
- Core philosophy line: **"We don't start with the technology. We investigate how the work actually gets done."**
